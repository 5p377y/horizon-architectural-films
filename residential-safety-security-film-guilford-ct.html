Safety & Security Film

Help protect your home's most vulnerable glass.

Ground-floor windows, sliding glass doors, and French doors near entry points are some of the easiest places for someone to get into a home. Safety and security film adds a tough, transparent layer that helps hold glass together if it's shattered — by a storm, an accident, or an attempted break-in — making it harder and slower to get through.

  • Helps hold glass together if it's shattered or broken
  • Adds resistance against storm debris and accidental impact
  • Makes forced entry through glass doors and windows more difficult
  • An affordable layer of protection for ground-floor glass

Good to Know

Peace of mind for the glass you don't think about.

It's easy to overlook how exposed sliding doors and ground-floor windows are until something happens. Safety film is an unobtrusive way to close that gap.
