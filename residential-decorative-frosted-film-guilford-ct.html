Decorative / Frosted Film

Style and privacy, in the same film.

Decorative and frosted films do double duty — they add a clean, upscale look to entryways, sidelights, glass doors, and shower enclosures, while also adding privacy. Available in a range of patterns and opacity levels, they're an easy way to upgrade plain glass without replacing it.

  • Adds a designer look to entryways, sidelights & glass doors
  • Available in a range of patterns and opacity levels
  • Pairs privacy with a finished, upscale appearance
  • A budget-friendly alternative to replacing glass

Good to Know

Great for the glass your guests notice first.

Front door sidelights, shower and bathroom glass, and glass-paneled interior doors are some of the most common places homeowners choose decorative film.
