Our Window Film Services

From privacy and style to energy savings and protection, we have the perfect film for your home or business.

Solar Control Film

Reduce heat, glare & UV — keep the view.

Solar control film cuts unwanted heat and glare while maintaining your view and natural light. It's one of the most effective ways to improve comfort and lower energy costs in rooms with big windows or all-day sun exposure.

  • Reduces heat and glare
  • Blocks up to 99% of harmful UV rays
  • Protects flooring, furniture & artwork from fading
  • Maintains natural light and outward visibility
Frosted & Decorative Film

Privacy and style, without losing the light.

Add privacy and a designer touch to bathrooms, entryways, sidelights, conference rooms, and storefronts with decorative frosted film — available in a range of patterns and opacity levels.

  • Increases privacy without sacrificing natural light
  • Decorative patterns & custom designs available
  • Great for bathrooms, entryways & sidelights
  • Enhances the look of your home or business
