Common Questions

Window Film Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about cost, process, warranty, and service area in Guilford, CT and the Connecticut Shoreline. Don't see your question? Just reach out.

General

How much does window film cost?

Pricing depends on the size and number of windows, the film type (solar control, frosted/decorative, safety & security, or SmartFilm switchable film), and the complexity of the installation. We provide free, no-obligation estimates after seeing your space.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Horizon Architectural Films is licensed and insured for residential and commercial window film installation.

What film brands do you install?

We're an authorized dealer for Global Express Window Films, Avery Dennison, and SmartFilm, giving us access to a wide range of solar control, safety & security, decorative/frosted, and switchable smart film products.

Is window film backed by a warranty?

Yes — our installations are backed by manufacturer warranties from the film brands we carry. Warranty terms vary by product, and we'll walk you through the specifics for the film you choose before installation.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We offer free in-home and on-site consultations and estimates for both residential and commercial projects.

Residential

Will window film make my home look dark or reflective from outside?

It depends on the film you choose. Many solar control films are designed to be nearly unnoticeable from outside while still cutting heat and glare indoors, and decorative/frosted films are chosen specifically for a visible, styled look. We'll show you samples so you know exactly what to expect before installing.

Can film be installed on any type of window?

Most residential windows are good candidates for film, including dual-pane and waterfront-facing windows. During your free consultation, we'll evaluate your specific windows and recommend the right product.

Will film damage my windows or void the window manufacturer's warranty?

Professionally installed, manufacturer-approved window film is designed not to damage glass. Some window manufacturers have their own film policies, so if your windows are still under warranty, it's worth checking your documentation — we're happy to help you review it.

Commercial

Do you work around business hours?

Yes. For commercial projects we coordinate scheduling around your operating hours to minimize disruption, including phased installation for larger buildings.

What commercial spaces do you work with?

Offices, retail storefronts, schools, and medical facilities are all spaces we regularly work in, with film solutions ranging from solar control and privacy to safety & security film.

Process & Installation

What does the installation process look like?

It starts with a consultation to understand your goals, followed by precise measurements, help choosing the right film, professional installation, and a final inspection to make sure everything looks right.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the project. Many residential jobs are completed in a single day; larger commercial installations may be scheduled across multiple days or phases.

Is there a mess, and do I need to be home?

Installation is clean and low-disruption — our team cleans up after the job. For residential projects we recommend someone be available for walkthroughs at the start and end; for commercial projects we coordinate access directly with your team.
